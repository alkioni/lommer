Services

  1. Bespoke products for a brand or company

  2. Corporate and event products

  3. Products made from a manufacturer’s own materials and offcuts

  4. Production management

  5. Material research

Most projects start with a practical question: what can be made from this material, how can this idea become a real object, how can this product be reworked.

We research (the brand, the user, the material etc.), we design the product and make the prototypes. Then we take charge of production with manufacturers in Europe and handle quality control, packaging and delivery.

Selected work

EvaFamily, our philosophy in action

Our own collection, launched in 2015 and including our award-winning pieces, shows the mentality behind our work.

We started from the material.

We chose E.V.A. for what it does: lightweight, durable, waterproof, easy to clean. In recent years we moved to E.V.A. with recycled content recovered from the supplier’s own production waste.

We built for strength.

We stitched perforated E.V.A. and it tore along the seam. So we developed a construction where flat E.V.A. is folded into shape and held together by snap buttons, made for us by YKK. There is not a single stitch.

We wasted less.

Every product was made to order, and leftover material became keyrings and bracelets. Packaging was designed to be reused too, in recycled paper or cotton fabric from a Greek producer.

Every product started from a single idea.

EvaPack

Bronze · International Design Awards 2017

We wanted a bag that protects its contents without a zip or a lock. A sailing rope passes through itself to form the handle, secure the contents and keep the shoulder straps even, with no extra parts. It is designed as an anti-theft solution.

EvaTwo

Silver · A’ Design Award, 2016 · Fashion & Travel Accessories Design

A lightweight tote made from a single sheet of E.V.A. and automotive seatbelt webbing. The strap carries the weight and the user can change it easily, to suit their mood.
