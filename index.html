What we do

We design physical products, from the first concept to a finished, delivered batch.

Most projects start with a practical question: what can be made from this material, how can this idea become a real object, how can this product be reworked.

We research the brand, the user and the material, design the product and make the prototypes. Then we take charge of production with manufacturers in Europe, and handle quality control, packaging and delivery.

  1. Bespoke products For a brand or company, from first concept to finished batch.

  2. Corporate & event products Objects and accessories for brand events, campaigns and clients.

  3. Upcycled products Made from a manufacturer’s own materials and production offcuts.

Our collection

EvaFamily, our philosophy in action

Our own collection, launched in 2015 and including our award-winning pieces, shows how we think and work.

We started from the material.

We chose E.V.A. for what it does: lightweight, durable, waterproof, easy to clean. In recent years we moved to E.V.A. with recycled content recovered from the supplier’s own production waste.

We built for strength.

Stitching perforated E.V.A. and it tore along the seam. So we developed a construction where flat E.V.A. is folded into shape and held together by snap buttons, made for us by YKK. There is not a single stitch.

We wasted less.

Every product was made to order, and leftover material became keyrings and bracelets. Packaging was designed to be reused too, in recycled paper or cotton fabric from a Greek producer.

Every product started from a single idea.

EvaPack

Bronze · International Design Awards 2017

We wanted a bag that protects its contents without a zip or a lock. A sailing rope passes through itself to form the handle, secure the contents and keep the shoulder straps even, with no extra parts. It is designed as an anti-theft solution.

Studio

Lommer is a product design studio based in Greece, founded in 2015. Since then we designed and produced our own collection of bags and accessories. Today we use that experience to design products for other companies.

The name stands for LOve Materials, MEet Requirements: we start from the material, then meet what the product has to do.

Lommer is led by Alkioni Matsourdeli, founder and product designer, who studied Product Design at Akto College (Middlesex University programme) and Interior Design at the Technical School of Athens.
