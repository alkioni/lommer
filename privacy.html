Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains what information lommer.design collects when you visit, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Lommer is a product design studio based in Greece. For any question about your data, write to hello@lommer.gr.

What we collect

Only if you click Accept in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed, how long visits last, the type of device and browser, the approximate location (country or city) and how you arrived at the site. We use this only in aggregate, to improve the site. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

If you click Decline, or do not choose, Google Analytics is not loaded and nothing is collected.

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply to you.
